The problem

You already know how to lift. The thinking is what costs you.

Which program. Which variation this block. When to push the max and when to hold. What to eat to make it count. Most serious lifters spend more hours deciding than training, and still second-guess every top set.

A coach decides.

Ascend writes your plan specifically for you, from your goal, your lifting history, your injuries, your equipment and your week. Every session, every load, every variation.

A coach watches.

Log a set and the loads move. Film a lift and the coach reads the bar path and your knees. Photograph a plate and the macros are read for you.

A coach explains.

Every Sunday: what it saw, what changed, why, and what happens next. In plain words, with the numbers in front. When life moves a session, the week reschedules intelligently around it.

Developed with world-class coaches

We have an opinion that works.

AI where it sees, our algorithm where it decides. AI reads your lifts on video and your meals from a photo, and the coach answers when you talk to it. But what you lift, when and how much is decided by a coaching system we wrote with coaches who get people strong for a living, not by a model guessing. It takes what works across methodologies, picks a training goal and a methodology that fit you, and writes the plan for you alone: your injuries, your lifting history, your schedule, your goal. Because the decisions are rules and not a prompt, they are consistent: the same evidence gets the same sound call every time. And the coach keeps guiding you while working around your life.

  • Several training goals and methodologies: strength, size, a meet to peak for, a comeback after an injury. The coach picks and adapts.
  • Loads come from your training max, never from how you feel today. No RPE guesswork.
  • A max moves at most 5 kg a week, and only when a logged top set earns it.
  • Miss a day and the week reschedules itself. Report pain and the next sessions swap around it. Get sick and the week compresses, then re-enters gently.
  • Changes land on upcoming sessions only. Your history is never rewritten.
