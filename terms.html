Please read this before you train with Ascend. By creating an account or paying for a membership you confirm that you have read it and agree to it.

1. What Ascend is

Ascend is a strength coaching app. It writes and adjusts a training plan, gives feedback on lifting technique from video, and helps you track food. It gives general training and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical service, and nothing in the app or said by anyone at Ascend is medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or physiotherapy.

2. Your health

You confirm that you are 18 or older and that, as far as you know, you are healthy enough for heavy resistance training. If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a recent injury or surgery, are pregnant, or have any other condition that could be affected by hard training or a change in diet, speak to a doctor before you start and follow their advice over anything Ascend suggests. Tell the app about anything that limits your training now, and keep that up to date.

3. Risk

Lifting weights carries a real risk of injury, including muscle and tendon tears, joint and back injuries and, rarely, serious injury or death. You take part voluntarily and you accept the risks that are a normal part of this kind of training.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Choose loads you can control. The numbers in your plan are suggestions, and you decide whether a lift is safe for you on the day.
  • Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath beyond normal effort, and get medical help.
  • Use equipment that is in good condition, use safety bars or a spotter on heavy lifts, and train in a suitable space.
  • Give accurate information about yourself, your lifts and your limitations.

5. Limits of the technology

Form feedback and meal estimates are produced with the help of AI from the video and photos you send. They can be wrong or incomplete. Form feedback is not a substitute for an in-person coach or a medical assessment, and meal estimates are approximate. Do not rely on them where an error could harm you, for example with a food allergy or a medical diet.

6. Founder access

If your membership includes direct contact with the founder, that contact is general training guidance based on what you share. It is not individual medical, physiotherapy or dietetic care, and it does not replace a professional who can examine you in person.

7. Results

Progress depends on your effort, recovery, nutrition and many things we do not control. We do not promise any specific result. The 30-day money-back guarantee for founding members applies as described on the membership page.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law, including liability for harm caused intentionally or by gross negligence, or any rights you have as a consumer.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change is material we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies to you.
