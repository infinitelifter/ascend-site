## 2. Your health
You confirm that you are 18 or older and that, as far as you know, you are healthy enough for heavy resistance training. If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a recent injury or surgery, are pregnant, or have any other condition that could be affected by hard training or a change in diet, speak to a doctor before you start and follow their advice over anything Ascend suggests. Tell the app about anything that limits your training now, and keep that up to date.

## 3. Risk
Lifting weights carries a real risk of injury, including muscle and tendon tears, joint and back injuries and, rarely, serious injury or death. You take part voluntarily and you accept the risks that are a normal part of this kind of training.

## 4. Your responsibilities
- Choose loads you can control. The numbers in your plan are suggestions, and you decide whether a lift is safe for you on the day.
- Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath beyond normal effort, and get medical help.
- Use equipment that is in good condition, use safety bars or a spotter on heavy lifts, and train in a suitable space.
- Give accurate information about yourself, your lifts and your limitations.

## 5. Limits of the technology

Form feedback is not a substitute for an in-person coach or a medical assessment, and meal estimates are approximate. Do not rely on them where an error could harm you, for example with a food allergy or a medical diet.
